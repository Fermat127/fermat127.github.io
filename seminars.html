Stable and Chromatic Homotopy Theory

Academic year 2026–2027.

The seminar begins with the foundations of stable homotopy theory, including spectra, filtered spectra, Eilenberg–MacLane spectra, the Steenrod algebra and the Adams spectral sequence. The later part of the seminar will move toward chromatic homotopy theory and the nilpotence theorem.
