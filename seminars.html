Stable and Chromatic Homotopy Theory

Academic year 2026–2027. We meet Thursdays from 10 to 12

The aim of the seminar is to work through Achim Krause's notes and understand Robert Burklund's modern proof of the nilpotence theorem using synthetic spectra.
